How to prepare for an AC service call

A clear description can help the service conversation get started. Tell the company whether the air conditioner runs, when you first noticed a change, and whether the issue is constant or comes and goes. If you have already checked the thermostat or power, mention that too.

Before the visit, you can:

  • Write down the symptoms and when they occur.
  • Make the thermostat and visible equipment information easy to access.
  • Clear a safe path to the equipment, if possible.
  • Keep track of any steps you have tried and whether they changed anything.

A professional can assess the concern and explain the options for addressing it. Ask what the recommended work is meant to fix, and speak up if anything is unclear. Do not assume the problem is gone just because the air conditioner begins running again for a short time.
