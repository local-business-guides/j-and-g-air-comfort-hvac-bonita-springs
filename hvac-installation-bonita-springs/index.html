What to discuss before installation

A professional installation starts with understanding the home and the equipment that is already there. Be ready to explain the system's age if you know it, the areas that feel uncomfortable, and whether the project is prompted by a breakdown or a planned upgrade. The contractor can evaluate the home and explain suitable next steps.

Use this checklist when preparing:

  • Make a note of comfort concerns in specific rooms.
  • Gather any information you have about the current system.
  • List questions about the equipment, proposed work, and operation.
  • Confirm what parts of the installation are included before work begins.
  • Ask what you should do to prepare the work area and home.
